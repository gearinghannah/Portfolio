Who am I?

I'm a recent First-Class Neuroscience with French graduate from the University of Manchester with a passion for scientific communication and and seeing how we can empower other to live healthier as we communicate science effectively and creatively.

Yes, I usually get a weird reaction to my course choice, but it matched pretty well with my intersectional thinking habits and global curiosity about how we can interpret neuroscience problems with an abstract approach.

I've grown up internationally and credit my multinationality for my adaptability and capacity to evolve to new environments quickly, as well as absorbing cultural nuances.

On top of my multicultural background, I undertook a placement year in Lyon, France, where I worked as a lab intern at the Institute NeuroMyoGène in a team investigating the metastatic dissemination routes of Group 3 medulloblastoma - essentially a very aggressive form of paediatric brain cancer that targets children ages between 4-16 years old, has high rates of mortality after relapse. The 9-month placement was absolutely the highlight of my university experience! I got to be a part of a great research team, gain invaluable lab experience, attend some amazing internal seminars and meet some lovely people!

I love getting stuck in to a new therapy area or gaining exposure to different public health questions. I am keen to start a professional career in science communications and would love to create engaging and accessible content for a wide range of audiences!
