ESSAY

BLOOD-BRAIN BARRIER

This was a practice essay as a part of my final-year tutorial unit. The question was regarding homeostasis and the cascade that follows after it is disturbed. I chose to focus on the blood-brain barrier (BBB) as I was also taking a neuroinflammation module, as well as my final-year project on ischaemic stroke.

This was part of the introduction where I describe what homeostasis is and how it is integral to our physiology. Additionally, I explain the elements of the BBB and its importance within the central nervous system.

This was a figure I adapted from another paper, illustrating the components within the BBB; the separate cells that are part of the network, connected by specific proteins. The right side of the schematic shows the disrupted BBB and the events that take place as a result of the disturbed homeostasis.

I received excellent feedback from my tutor and enjoyed summarising the details of the BBB in relation to neurodegeneration and injury. As I was doing a few other modules within the scope of neuroinflammation, it was a good exercice to refresh my communication skills for this disease area.
