MODERN NEUROSCIENCE COMMUNICATION

The Nervous Breakdown

I started a science communications project based on Instagram as a way to reach a younger audience that is increasingly using social media as a form of reliable media. As more traditional media, like newsletters and journals, lose their hold on the attention of young viewers, it is important to create new ways to reach them, and to keep them well-informed about the true scientific evidence, especially in this era of widespread misinformation online.

My aim is to gently introduce areas of neuroscience and novel therapies discovered to younger audiences, using cultural references and iconography that makes it more accessible and fun to engage with!

We keep hearing more and more about a decrease in attention spans, which is true to a certain extent, but I am a firm believer in empowering people to keep learning and staying curious about the world around them! In my case, I'd like to help others learn more about themselves through neuroscience, and for science to be a way for them to practice analytical thinking skills. Science communicated through social media is the next wave of science communication and I know it has the potential to ignite the next generation of researchers!
