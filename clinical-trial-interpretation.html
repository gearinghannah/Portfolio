MEDICAL AFFAIRS

NWBI MEDICAL COMMUNICATIONS COMPETITION

I created and presented this deck as part of the NorthWest Biotech Institute (NWBI) Medical Communications Competition 2026. I presented the phase 4 clinical trial findings for Rimegepant to treat migraine in triptan-unsuitable patients. I described the trial design, primary and secondary endpoints and safety considerations that were outlined in the report. I achieved second place after successfully answering questions from the panel of judges, including industry professionals, regarding the details of the trials and future directions.

The Concept

The main aim for this task was to present how Rimegepant was a better alternative, specifically for the underserved population of people who experience migraines and are triptan-unsuitable.

I started by explaining what the current first-line migraine treatments are, additional what barriers the triptan-unsuitable patients face as a result. From there, I showed the differences in the pharmacological mechanism and how this offers an alternative therapeutic strategy.

The Process

I decided to use a visual demonstration of the pharmacological mechanisms displaying how rimegepant and triptans both act upon the same metabolic pathway, but target separate components, allowing triptan-unsuitable patients, with higher risk of complications due to vasoconstriction, to treat their migraines.

In the next slides, I clearly stated the result of the primary endpoint, which showed a significant improvement in migraine management and pain relief agter Rimegepant compared to the placebo. And, after showing the results of the secondary endpoints and observed adverse effects, I continued to suggest the future implications of this novel treatment option for triptan-unsuitable patients and how to interpret these phase 4 findings with the unpublished extended trial results.

The presentation was awarded second place, and I received great feedback regarding my clear understanding of the trial design, as well as my confidence in presenting and answering questions from the judges.
